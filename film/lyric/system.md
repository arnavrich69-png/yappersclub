# The lyric film system, for people who have never heard the legend

Brief 04 builds every lyric film on "every song is a new flavour of imli". Few viewers know why
Dhwanikul is an imli, and a film that needs a footnote loses them in the first second. So the
system keeps the brand's world and drops the need to know where it comes from.

## The rules

1. **The lyric comes first.** Every scene is designed from what the singer is saying (stage 3 asks
   "what is the singer saying, and how does the thread show it?"). The candy, the label and the
   thread are how we show it, never what we show instead of it.
2. **Nothing needs the legend.** The films never mention Tansen or his tree unless the song does.
   Everything on screen works at face value:
   - the wrapper is a sweet with a flavour: a mood anyone gets (खट्टा मीठा, sour and sweet);
   - the thread is a string you can play, and a kalava that ties people together, which everyone
     in India already knows from temples and rakhi;
   - the label world's jokes (ingredients, best before, stamps) are packet jokes, universal.
3. **The legend lives in one place.** The pinned reel इमली क्यों? (`film/out/imli-film.mp4`) tells it
   once, in 21 seconds. Captions can point to it; films never have to.
4. **Every film opens on its title, as a flavour.** The first 1.5 seconds: the wrapper untwists to the
   song's label: FLAVOUR No. XX, the Hindi flavour name, the three word English line, the song's name
   and the artist. A viewer learns what they are watching before any metaphor arrives.
5. **One idea a moment, readable at speed.** One hero word, one support line of 7 words or fewer, on
   screen at least 1.5 s. Hindi heroes in Modak, English support in Big Shoulders.
6. **Every film ends the same way.** The thread ties off and the label reads FLAVOUR NAME · ARTIST ·
   ध्वनि KUL, so the brand is learned by repetition, not explanation.

## What changes in brief 04's stages

| Stage | Change |
|-------|--------|
| 1 and 2 | Automated and measured: `npm run lyric:timing`, `lyric:analyse`, `lyric:sync` (see `README.md`) |
| 3 | The creative direction also answers: does any scene need the legend to make sense? If so, redesign it |
| 4 and 5 | The flavour title opener and the tie off ending are built once and reused by every film |

## What stays

The three worlds (LABEL, LANTERN, PULP), red for the thread alone, the thread's vocabulary, Modak,
Big Shoulders and Khand with correct shaping, the safe zones, and the PYAAR? discipline: freeze what
is approved, review pictures not code, prove small first.
